=== VIDEO FACTS (transcript) ===
- Open-source video generator, runs LOCALLY, offline, free, unlimited.
- As low as 5-6 GB VRAM can run it. Audio built-in (synced stereo). Great character consistency, world knowledge, instruction following, music sync.
- Multimodal: takes images, video, audio as references.
- Model sizes: full 66GB -> int8 34GB -> pruned FP8 or pruned int8 = 21GB. Users report running pruned on as low as 12GB VRAM. "if you have even lower VRAM, don't worry."
- Text encoder = Qwen3-VL-32B ("Quinn 3VL32B"). "this doesn't have to fit on your VRAM" (CPU/RAM offload OK).
- Ref2Video workflow uses "Gemma 32B" for load clip (alt encoder path).
- VAE: download BOTH audio VAE and video VAE.
- Default steps = 20. ~5 min per gen on recent Nvidia GPU.
- Workflows: T2V (text-to-video), I2V (image-to-video, first/last frame), R2V (reference-to-video, most powerful — images/video/audio refs).
- R2V: up to 9 ref images, 3 ref videos (own soundtrack), 3 standalone ref audio. ref_image_size match=fast, max=2048px short edge for fidelity.
